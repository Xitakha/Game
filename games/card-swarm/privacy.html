Privacy and personal data processing policy of Swarm Hand

Version 2026-11-14a · Effective from 14 November 2026

Current status: in this version of the game, sending statistics is switched off; the game sends nothing to any service. This policy describes what will happen, always with your prior permission, once it is switched on.

1. Who is responsible

Alex Jacome, based in Bogotá, Colombia. Contact for anything related to your data: raico29@gmail.com.

Until we have a representative in the European Union, the game does not send statistics from devices set to a European time zone (and does not even show you the notice).

2. Scope

Swarm Hand as published on GitHub Pages (including the installable version) and on itch.io. On game portals (for example CrazyGames or Poki), Swarm Hand sends no data to any service of ours; the portal's policy applies there.

3. What data we process and why

  • Without your permission we send nothing. The game stores your progress and settings on your device so it can work; that never leaves it.
  • With your permission ("I accept" button in the notice, or the switch in Settings > Privacy), the game sends gameplay statistics: which area and danger level you play, how long a run lasts, how it ends, which bosses appear and who wins, which fusions and purchases you make, the graphics quality in use, device type (phone, tablet or computer), language (Spanish, English or other), game version, technical errors, the date of your first game and how many days have passed since, the version of the notice you accepted, and a random code that is only valid for the current session.
  • We do not send your name, email, age, precise location or any identifier that follows you across sessions or devices. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not profile you and we make no automated decisions about you.
  • Your IP address and the technical data your browser always sends (for example browser type) reach our provider with each transmission; we will configure it not to store the IP or the location derived from it.
  • Purpose: understanding which parts of the game are fun, which are too hard, where people stop playing and which errors occur, in order to improve the game.
  • These statistics do not directly identify you; even so, we handle them with the safeguards that apply to personal data.
  • On your device, and only if you accept, the game also stores the date of your first game, some counters, the events waiting to be sent and the codes of your latest 10 sessions (so you can exercise your rights). All of it can be deleted in Settings > Privacy.

4. Legal basis

Your prior, express and informed consent (Colombian Law 1581 of 2012; Article 6(1)(a) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR; and rules on access to your device, such as Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC). Giving it is optional and changes nothing in the game. You can withdraw it at any time as easily as you gave it; withdrawal does not affect data already sent. Every event sent carries the version of the notice you accepted, as proof of your authorization.

5. Minors

Swarm Hand is not directed at minors. If you are under 18, do not accept sending statistics; the game works the same. If we learn that a minor's data was sent, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child accepted, contact us (section 8).

6. Who receives the data

Our analytics provider, PostHog Inc., as data processor, with the data hosted in its European Union region (Germany). Before switching sending on we will formalize its data processing agreement, which includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for any access from outside the EU. Sending data from Colombia to the EU is an international transmission of data to a processor. We share the data with no one else, unless required by law.

7. How long we keep it

Event-level statistics are kept for at most 13 months and then deleted. Aggregated totals (for example "win rate against a boss") may be kept because they no longer relate to anyone. What your device stores stays there until you delete it.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to know, access, update, correct and delete your data, to request proof of your consent, to be informed of how we use it, to withdraw your consent, to object and to restrict processing, and to portability where applicable. Exercising them is free.

  • In the game: Settings > Privacy lets you withdraw permission, see what is sent and delete the analytics data on your device.
  • At our provider: since we keep no data that identifies you, we need your help to find yours. In Settings > Privacy you will see the codes of your latest sessions; copy them and send them to raico29@gmail.com saying what you want us to do. Without those codes we cannot find your data, because we have no way of knowing which data is yours.
  • Response times: we answer queries within 10 business days and complaints within 15 business days at most. If that is not possible, we will tell you why and when we will answer, within the limits allowed by law.
  • Complaints: you can complain to Colombia's Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (www.sic.gov.co) or, if you live in the European Union, to your country's data protection authority. We would appreciate it if you contacted us first so we can try to solve it.

9. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). The game uses no cookies for analytics and does not load the provider's tracking library; it only sends the data described above.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, the game will ask for your permission again before sending anything under the new terms. Previous versions are available on request.
